The panel

8 knobs · 2 minis · 2 encoders · SMASH · BYPASS

Fixed knobs, no paging: each big knob is one block. Turning a knob jumps the screen to that block's page.

Knob 1 · HPF
Off (10 Hz) up to 16 kHz, then closed to silence in the last 3%.
Knob 2 · DRV A
Clean bypass to +36 dB into the curve (+48 dB on FUZZ, DIST and METAL).
Knob 3 · SHP A
Drive A's character, different on every type.
Knobs 4, 5 · DRV B, SHP B
The second drive, in series.
Knob 6 · LPF
Closed (silent) to off (22 kHz).
Knob 7 · MOTION
First half: depth and wet to full. Second half: feedback to max.
Knob 8 · MIX
Dry to wet.
Mini left · RESO
Fades the resonator in, then raises its decay; the top self-oscillates.
Mini right · SQUASH
Transparent to +24 dB into the compressor and clipper.
FREEZE · SMASH
While held: everything slammed. Its gate jack does the same.
SYNC · BYPASS
Press to toggle a latency-matched bypass. Both buttons can be reassigned.
02

Drives

Tape · Tanh · Tube · Seal · Fuzz · Metal · Fold · Wrap · XOR · crushers

Two drive stages in series inside 4x linear-phase oversampling. DRV always gets louder as it goes up: nothing auto-compensates, and SQUASH catches the top. Each stage has OUTPUT, TONE, BIAS, STAGE MIX and a STEREO mode that can drive only the sides, so kick and bass stay clean while the width gets dirty.

03

Filters

MS-20 style HPF · audio FM · stereo spread · clean LPF

  • HPFMS-20 style

    Resonance feeds back through an asymmetric diode-style clipper, so it howls and dirties when pushed. Self-oscillates from about 80% RES. 12 or 24 dB.

  • Audio FM

    The input audio moves its own cutoff at audio rate: gritty sidebands, and the cutoff jumps with every hit. SELF, CROSS or MONO sources make the stereo field wobble.

  • Spread

    Pushes the left and right cutoffs apart, up to 2 octaves each way, and SPREAD FM throws them around at audio rate for chaotic width even from a mono mix.

  • Filters = all

    The dry path is filtered too, so the HPF works like a DJ filter at any MIX.

  • LPF

    A clean SVF after the drives, 20 Hz to 20 kHz, 12 or 24 dB, closing to silence at the bottom.

05

Squash

Glue · slam · soft · tanh · hard · tape · fold

The master section after MIX, so it acts on the blended signal, dry included. The knob pushes 0 to +24 dB into a compressor (OFF, GLUE or SLAM) and then a master clipper (SOFT, TANH, HARD, TAPE or FOLD), with a ceiling and output level. Near-silence is left alone.

06

CV outs & meters

Envelopes · transients · bite · LFOs · chaos · LED level meters

Six CV outs that follow the music, each with its own source, polarity and offset, and two clock outs that light as fast level meters or fire gates.

Envelopes
The whole mix, the kick band, the mids, the hats and air, or the stereo difference, in dB so quiet parts still move it.
Transients
A decay fired by each hit, or a new random value on each hit.
Bite and squash
How hard the drives are clipping, and SQUASH's gain reduction.
LFOs and chaos
The MOTION LFO, a free sine, or a Lorenz attractor's X and Y.
Clock L / R
LED level meters (input or output), a 10 ms pulse per hit, or a gate while the drives clip hard.
07

Buttons, presets & saving

Smash · bypass · kill lows · mute · 8 slots · autosave

  • Buttons

    Each button and its gate jack can SMASH, BYPASS, KILL LOWS or MUTE, momentary or latching.

  • Presets

    Eight slots store every page setting: types, values, CV bases, the output map and button actions. Knobs stay live.

  • Autosave

    Settings save themselves three seconds after your last change and come back at power-up.

  • CV in

    Kali's big knobs are attenuators on their CV jacks, so each knob gets a CV BASE setting to add an offset under patched CV.

Install

The download includes the firmware, the Daisy bootloader, a Mac flash script and these steps.

First install (Mac)

  1. Install dfu-util once (brew install dfu-util) and plug Kali straight into the computer with a USB data cable, no hub.
  2. Unzip the download and run ./flash-kali.sh --first KaliSmash-….bin in that folder.
  3. When it asks: hold BOOT, press and release RESET, release BOOT. It installs the bootloader, catches it as it starts and installs KALI SMASH. No timing on your side.

Updates (any computer)

  1. On the module: SYSTEM → UPDATE, turn to GO. It waits for an upload.
  2. In Chrome, open flash.daisy.audio and choose File Upload.
  3. Pick the .bin and the Daisy Bootloader device (not "DFU in FS Mode"). On a Mac, ./flash-kali.sh KaliSmash-….bin does the same.

Status: beta. Every block has been played on hardware; polish is still going on. The first install currently needs a Mac. To go back to stock, flash Kali's v2.22 image the factory way (hold BOOT, tap RESET, File Upload, "DFU in FS Mode"), which also removes the Daisy bootloader.
