BRIBELUGE COMPANION

A second screen for the Synthstrom Deluge on a phone, tablet or laptop, over USB. The display spelled out in full, knobs and engine drawings, waveforms with draggable markers, a song view you can tap, and a panel for every engine, drawn like the machine it comes from. Built on the Deluge Community firmware.

Launch Companion Chrome or Edge. Works with Bribeluge v1.32+ and stock community firmware 1.3.

A panel for every engine

Each engine gets its own panel, drawn like the machine it comes from. Drag a control and the Deluge follows; turn a knob on the Deluge and the panel moves. Touch a control and the Deluge lights the gold knobs that turn it; with SHORTCUT lit it opens that setting too.

Synths

The Deluge's own synth modes and the synth engines Bribeluge adds.

Drum machines

Whole kits: every row is one of the machine's voices, and the keys along the bottom pick a row and play it.

Drum voices

Single engines for one kit row, alongside samples or any other kit's voices.

Connect your Deluge

Pick your device. The app opens on its own platform's steps.

  1. Plug in. Run a USB cable from the Deluge's USB port to your phone or tablet. Use a USB-C (or OTG) cable or adapter that carries data: some cables only charge.
  2. Launch in Chrome. Open this page in Chrome and tap Launch Companion. Other Android browsers may not support Web MIDI.
  3. Tap Connect in the top bar, then Allow when Chrome asks to control your MIDI devices.
  4. You're live. The Deluge port is picked for you and the light turns green. Any of the Deluge's ports works.
  5. Optional: install it. In the app, Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen. It then runs fullscreen, keeps the screen awake and reconnects on its own.
  1. Plug in. Connect the Deluge's USB port to your computer with a data USB cable.
  2. Free the port. Close any other app using the Deluge's MIDI (a DAW, an editor): on Windows a MIDI port usually opens in one app at a time.
  3. Launch in Chrome or Edge. Click Launch Companion. Firefox and other browsers may not support Web MIDI with SysEx.
  4. Click Connect in the top bar, then Allow when the browser asks to control your MIDI devices.
  5. You're live. The Deluge port is picked for you and the light turns green. If not, choose it in the port list.
  1. Plug in. Connect the Deluge's USB port to your Mac with a data USB cable (or a USB-C adapter).
  2. Launch in Chrome or Edge. Click Launch Companion. Safari doesn't support Web MIDI.
  3. Click Connect in the top bar, then Allow when the browser asks to control your MIDI devices.
  4. You're live. The Deluge port is picked for you and the light turns green. Your DAW can keep using the Deluge at the same time.

Firmware: everything works with Bribeluge v1.32 or later. Plain community firmware 1.3 works in STOCK mode after a one-time MIDI Follow setup, listed in the app once you pick STOCK. The STOCK / BRIBELUGE switch picks itself when the Deluge answers.

Built on the community firmware

The companion stands on work the Deluge community did first.

  • Deluge Community firmware. Synthstrom Audible open-sourced the Deluge firmware, and the community keeps it going at SynthstromAudible/DelugeFirmware (GPL-3.0). Bribeluge is a fork of it, and the companion talks to the Deluge through features it added.
  • Screen over SysEx, by bfredl. The OLED and 7SEG mirror the companion shows is the community firmware's display-over-SysEx feature (2023), first shown in bfredl's delugeclient. The companion unpacks the same stream: 7-to-8 bit packing with run-length encoding, the inverse of the firmware's pack_8to7_rle.
  • DEx, by silicakes. The best general Deluge web client (MIT), and the reference for how the companion drives the mirror (keep-alive, display swap). No DEx code is included; the companion is written from scratch.
  • MIDI Follow. A community firmware feature. In STOCK mode every knob in the companion is a MIDI Follow CC, using the firmware's default map.
  • What Bribeluge adds. Extra SysEx messages under the same header, for track state, kit rows, pickers, the browser, waveforms, params, transport, clips, the arranger and the synth panels. These only exist in Bribeluge.
  • Licence. The companion is Bribery's own code, licensed under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 plus two additional permissions: you can make and sell music with it, and Synthstrom Audible may use it commercially with credit. Free to use, study, change and share; don't sell it. The Bribeluge firmware it talks to is a separate program and stays GPL-3.0.

How it talks to the Deluge

For troubleshooting and anyone building their own client.

Transport
Plain USB MIDI, through the browser's Web MIDI with SysEx permission. No OSC, no NRPN, no custom USB driver. Any of the Deluge's USB MIDI ports works.
Header
Community SysEx with Synthstrom's manufacturer ID: F0 00 21 7B 01 02 … F7, where 02 is the display command. Older docs and clients show F0 7D: that was the developer ID the community used until February 2024, when it moved to the official Synthstrom ID.
Screen mirror
Out: 00 02 keep alive, 00 03 force a full frame, 00 04 swap 7SEG / OLED. In: 40 OLED frame (packed + RLE, full or a page range), 41 7SEG digits. Works on stock community firmware 1.3.
Bribeluge state
Out: 03 <mode> after the header (subscribe, keep alive, picker, browser, SELECT / BACK, markers, pads, transport, clips, panels). In: 42 to 4F and 56 to 58. Bribeluge only.
Knobs in STOCK
MIDI Follow CCs on the Follow channel, with the Deluge's feedback coming back the same way.
Tempo and play light
MIDI clock from the Deluge: tempo from 24 ticks per beat, play from start / continue / stop.
Full message list 
Header           F0 00 21 7B 01 02 ... F7   (Synthstrom ID, product 01, display command 02)

Display mirror (community firmware)
  out  00 02                keep alive
  out  00 03                force a full frame
  out  00 04                swap 7SEG / OLED
  in   40 00 ...            OLED frame, packed 7-to-8
  in   40 01 ...            OLED frame, packed 7-to-8 + RLE
  in   40 02 <page> ...     OLED pages from <page>, packed + RLE
  in   41 00 00 <hi> d1-d4  7SEG: a high-bits byte, then 4 digit bytes

Bribeluge companion (Bribeluge only)
  out  03 00                subscribe and send everything
  out  03 01                keep alive
  out  03 02 <n>            choose picker option
  out  03 03 <hi> <lo>      move the browser
  out  03 04 / 03 05        press SELECT / BACK
  out  03 06 <m> <hi> <lo>  drag a waveform marker
  out  03 07 <x> <y>        open a shortcut pad's setting
  out  03 08 <cmd>          top bar: 0 play, 1 record, 2 select, 3 back, 4 left, 5 right
  out  03 09 <hi> <lo>      launch or stop a session clip
  out  03 0A <section>      launch a section
  out  03 0D <id> <hi> <lo> move a BRUNO106 panel control
  out  03 0E <id> <hi> <lo> move a BROPHET panel control
  out  03 0F <id> <hi> <lo> move a WAVE panel control
  out  03 14 <panel> <id> <hi> <lo>  move a control on any other panel (4 to 15)
  out  03 15 <row>          select a kit row
  out  03 16 <row> <on>     hold or release a kit row's audition
  out  03 17 <panel> <id>   a control was touched: light its gold knobs
  out  03 18 <panel> <id>   the same, and open its setting (SHORTCUT)
  in   42 STATE   43 KIT_ROWS   44 TEXT      45 PICKER     46 BROWSER
  in   47 BROWSER_SEL           48 WAVEFORM  49 PLAYHEAD   4A PARAMS
  in   4B TRANSPORT  4C OUTPUTS 4D CLIPS     4E ARRANGER   4F ARRANGER_POS
  in   56 PANEL (every engine)   57 WTABLE   58 WTPOS

The firmware side is in hid_sysex.cpp on the Bribeluge branch. The app is one unminified HTML file: open it and view source.
