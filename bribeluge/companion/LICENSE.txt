## What this covers

Bribery's own Bribeluge Companion work: the web app (`index.html`, `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`), its engine drawings, panels, icons and images, and the docs in this repo. All of it was written by Bribery.

## What this does not cover

- **The Bribeluge firmware**, including the firmware side of the Companion's SysEx messages. It is a fork of the Deluge Community firmware and stays GPL-3.0: https://github.com/thisisbribery/deluge-bribes-v1
- **The Deluge Community firmware's display-over-SysEx and MIDI Follow features**, which the Companion talks to. They belong to their authors under the firmware's GPL-3.0 licence. The Companion is a separate program that only exchanges MIDI messages with the Deluge; it contains no firmware code.
- **Other Deluge clients.** No code from bfredl's delugeclient or silicakes' DEx is included. They are credited in the README for showing how the display mirror works.
