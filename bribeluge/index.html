01

Drum machines

LXR · DXIA · DIIA · Cyc engines · GIN · ULTRAKIK · Autofill · Patterns

Synthesised drums on any kit row. A drum synth row is still a normal Deluge sound: its envelopes, filters, FX, mod matrix, automation and parameter locks all work on top. Every engine is in one list, SYNTH MODE, with Cycles-style gold knobs and one pad layout.

The engines

  • LXR voicesFrom LXR-02

    Kick / tom, Snare and Cymbal / hat, ported from the drum voices of the open source LXR-02 firmware by Erica Synths.

    DRUM Kick / tomSNAR SnareMETL Cymbal / hat
    Manual →

  • DXIAFM drums

    Three sine operators and a carrier, phase modulating each other in three routings. Each starts on a preset: a tight kick, a knocky kick, a long deep kick.

    DX-LDX-BDX-C
    Manual →

  • DIIAMetallic

    Inharmonic, metallic partials with a noise strike, in three variants: a snare-ish noise perc, a zap and metallic noise.

    DI-GDI-IDI-H
    Manual →

  • Cyc enginesCycles-style

    Eight engines modelled on the Model:Cycles' six machines and the Syntakt's SD VINTAGE and CP VINTAGE, with the same knobs and starting values, rebuilt from measurements. Running on integer table oscillators for speed.

    KIKSNRCMTLPERCTONECHRDVSNRCLAP

  • GINPO-32 style

    The drum voice from SquareWave Studio's GIN for the OP-1, built on libpo32. A tone with a pitch sweep, LFO or noise modulator, plus a filtered noise layer, mixed, driven and EQ'd. Its kits are built into the firmware.

    GIN

  • ULTRAKIKKick engine

    One swept oscillator into a wavefolder, four kinds of drive, grit, a click and a clean sub, with sixteen presets from clean to fully broken. Long gliding 808s, hard 909s, destroyed kicks.

Playing them

  • Kit presetsSHIFT + KIT

    Ready-made drum synth kits: Cycles kits, 42 LXR kits and the GIN kits built into the firmware. Rows tint by engine.

    Manual →

  • Kit pattern mode

    24 saved patterns for each of a kit's bottom six rows. Step a row through them or load a whole bank.

    Manual →

  • AutofillTAP

    TAP plays a fill to the end of the bar on Kick, Snare and Hihat 1: Ghost, Drive, Build, Climb or Stutter. Your patterns are never changed.

    Manual →

  • Affect allSHIFT + AFFECT ENTIRE

    Snapshot any kit: every knob moves all its sound rows (drum synths, samples and synths) and snaps back on the next bar.

    Manual →

  • Gate out

    A kit's bottom four rows fire the four gate outputs. GATE and MIDI out sit at the top of each row's menu.

    Manual →

  • MIDI kitsSHIFT + MIDI

    An MKIT: sixteen MIDI rows that kit patterns and Autofill can play, for driving external drum machines.

02

Synths

303 · BRIBERING (Rings) · BROPHET · BRUNO106 · Synth browser

Four new synth engines that play through the Deluge's own envelopes, filters, mod FX, delay, reverb and sidechain, plus a browser that puts hundreds of presets one press away.

  • 303 synthFrom Open303

    A TB-303 voice with accent and slide from the mono sequencer, three filters (303, LP18 and the pedal-style DIRT) and an overdrive after them.

    Manual →

  • BRIBERING MODEAfter Mutable Rings

    A resonator engine on synths and kit rows. Each note strikes a resonator that rings on in its own time, like a bell, a bar or a string. Five models, and Bow to keep it sounding.

    BMOD ModalBSYM SympatheticBSTR StringBFM FMBCHD Chord
    Manual →

  • BROPHETFrom Pro-54

    A two-oscillator poly synth from Pro-54, the open source port of NI's Pro-53: sync, poly mod, analog drift, its filter in every LPF list, and all 512 Pro-53 programs plus 24 Bribes presets.

    Manual →

  • BRUNO106From KR-106

    A Juno-style poly synth from Ultramaster KR-106: one DCO with saw, pulse, sub and noise, its IR3109 filter in every LPF list, the four position HPF with bass boost, and 212 presets.

    Manual →

  • Synth browserSHIFT + SYNTH

    Pick a type (SUBT, FM, WAVE, RING, BRINGS, DX7, BROPHET, BRUNO106, 303, INIT), then a category, then play through the presets. The audition pads play each type's init synth (or your own patch) the whole way. 99 wavetable, 288 DX7, 37 BRINGS, 536 BROPHET and 212 BRUNO106 presets included.

    Manual →

  • Feedback

    Feeds each synth voice's filter output back into its input: thicker, driven low end, and self-oscillation from patch cables.

    Manual →

  • Pan spread

    Each note of a poly synth gets its own place in the stereo field, like a poly synth's voice pan spread, no unison needed.

    Manual →

Install

Three steps from the SD card. To go back, install an official firmware file the same way.

  1. Download the latest .bin and copy it to the top level of the Deluge's SD card. Remove any other .bin files from the top level.
  2. Turn the Deluge off.
  3. Hold SHIFT and switch the Deluge on. Let go once it starts loading the new firmware.

Status: experimental. Made on a 7-segment Deluge; the OLED screens haven't been checked on hardware. Back up your SD card before installing. Optional preset folders (kits, wavetables, DX7, BRINGS, BROPHET, BRUNO106) are listed in the download notes.

Why

The thinking behind it.

Almost a brand new ultra version of what the Deluge has been for me since about 2018.

Eight deep tracks

Not working towards sixteen tracks at once or a mini DAW. A really refined 8-track machine, with an exceptional amount of capability within those 8 tracks.

Performance first

Super optimised and performance friendly, with the features missing since the Deluge was first released. It isn't built for everyone, and backwards compatibility isn't the priority, though older songs tested so far load without errors.
