Firmware

Unofficial mods for hardware I play. More wonk, more destruction, more speed.

  • 01

    Bribeluge

    Synthstrom Deluge

    Custom firmware rebased on the latest official community release (CFW v1.3.0): stumble swing, Autofill, a mono bassline sequencer, LXR, Cycles-style and GIN drum synths, a 303, Rings, a Pro-54 and a Juno, saturation and crush types.

    Download
  • 02

    Bribeluge Companion

    Phone or tablet + Deluge

    A second screen over USB. The display mirrored and spelled out, a drawing of each engine, picker tiles, waveforms and song view. Works in Chrome, with a STOCK mode for plain firmware.

    Become a beta tester
  • 03

    Seal Eggs

    Sealegs delay

    A browser flasher for the Sealegs: live reverse delay, Digi doppler, a second character on NOISE, the Seal Beads granular mode and the Seal Microloops micro-looper.

  • 04

    Stolperbeats Ultra

    Making Sound Machines Stolperbeats

    Alt swing tables, ratchets, a pitch per step and on-demand fills, written into your own copy of firmware 163 and flashed over USB.

    Timing visualiser
  • 05

    Bribestages

    Mutable Instruments Stages

    qiemem's bipolar firmware plus segment linking from the buttons, Follow segments (an envelope follower on any segment) and Sync LFOs, a bank of LFOs locked to one clock.

    Download
  • 06

    Kali Smash

    dsp.coffee Kali

    A stereo end-of-chain mix-buss destroyer: an MS-20 style HPF, two drives with 15 types, motion and resonators, SQUASH, and six CV outs that follow the music.

    Download beta
