Firmware

Unofficial mods for hardware I play. More wonk, more destruction, more speed.

  • 01

    Bribeluge

    Synthstrom Deluge

    Custom firmware on the community beta: stumble swing, Autofill, a mono bassline sequencer, LXR, Cycles-style and GIN drum synths, a 303, Rings, a Pro-54 and a Juno, saturation and crush types.

  • 02

    Bribeluge Companion

    Phone or tablet + Deluge

    A second screen over USB. The display mirrored and spelled out, a drawing of each engine, picker tiles, waveforms and song view. Works in Chrome, with a STOCK mode for plain firmware.

  • 03

    Seal Eggs

    Sealegs delay

    A browser flasher for the Sealegs: live reverse delay, Digi doppler, a second character on NOISE, the Seal Beads granular mode and the Seal Microloops micro-looper.

  • 04

    Stolperbeats Ultra

    Making Sound Machines Stolperbeats

    Alt swing tables, ratchets, a pitch per step and on-demand fills, written into your own copy of firmware 163 and flashed over USB.

  • 05

    Qiemem Bribery Edition

    Mutable Instruments Stages

    qiemem's bipolar firmware plus segment linking from the buttons and Follow segments, an envelope follower on any segment.

Unofficial, modified firmware. Use it at your own risk. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any of the hardware makers.
