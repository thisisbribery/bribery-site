Mutable Instruments Stages / v1.0 / formerly Qiemem Bribery Edition

BRIBESTAGES

qiemem's bipolar multi-mode firmware v1.3.0 with two additions: segment linking from the buttons, and Follow segments, an envelope follower on any segment. Everything qiemem's firmware does still works.

Update from this page Linking Follow

Why

Built for playing live.

Groups that stay put

On stock Stages, the gate jacks decide how segments group together. Playing live, a cable going in or out in the middle of a set would split or merge envelopes without warning. Linking sets the groups with the buttons, so the patch can change without the envelopes changing.

A follower in the case

Stages segments can already read audio through their CV jacks, so any segment can now be an envelope follower, without giving up a module for one.

Linking

In the Stages, Advanced and Slow LFO modes, with a single module. Links are stored.

Hold A, tap B
Links every segment from A to B. Triple green flash. Works right to left too. If they're already linked, sets a loop from A to B, same as stock.
Hold A, hold B for 2 s
Unlinks: every segment from A to B stands alone. Triple red flash. Unlinking the middle of a group splits it in two.
Short press
Changes segment type. The link stays.
Hold 0.5 s, release
Toggles loop (qiemem timing).
Hold 5 s, one button
Mode change, as in qiemem. With two buttons held, mode change is blocked, so a long link gesture can't switch modes by accident.
Hold + slider or pot
qiemem's property changes, unchanged.

Gates and outputs

  • A group comes out of its leftmost segment's output.
  • The leftmost segment's gate fires the whole group.
  • Gates patched into the other segments of a group are ignored, so patching them doesn't split or restart it.
  • A segment that isn't linked follows its own gate.

Free-running groups

  • With no gate in the first segment, a looped group runs by itself, like a multi-stage LFO.
  • A group without a loop waits for a gate.
  • Linked segments do a quick triple yellow flash every 4 seconds, over their normal colours.

Follow

Patch audio into a segment's CV jack and its output becomes the envelope of that audio.

Making one: tap the segment's button past the last type. Stages and Slow LFO modes: ramp, step, hold, follow. Advanced mode: ramp, step, hold, random, follow.

CV jack
Audio in. DC is blocked, so an offset doesn't read as level.
Slider
Release, 5 ms at the bottom to 5 s at the top.
Pot
Attack, instant fully left to 250 ms fully right.
Hold button + slider
Input gain in four zones, bottom to top: x1, x2, x4, x8. At x1 a 5 V audio peak gives full output. Use x4 or x8 for line-level sources.
Hold button + pot
Invert: the envelope goes negative, 0 V down to -8 V.
Gate jack
High freezes the envelope where it is. Low lets it follow again.
Output
0 to 8 V, or 0 to -8 V when inverted.
Lights
The button light is a level meter: green, then yellow, then red as the input gets louder. With no sound it breathes dim yellow (dim red when inverted). The slider light follows the output.

Good to know: a Follow segment always stands alone; links into or out of it are kept and come back when you change its type. With a very short release, low notes ripple, so raise the release to smooth it.

Update

Stages updates from audio. Its updater is built in and can't be damaged by a failed attempt.

You need: a computer or phone with a headphone jack (a wired USB headphone adapter works, Bluetooth doesn't) and a 3.5 mm patch cable. Play the file below from this page, or download it and play it in any player.

NormalModule mounted the right way up

.wav

FlippedModule mounted upside down

.wav
  1. Power the rack off. Unplug every cable from the Stages.
  2. Patch the headphone out into the CV input of the rightmost segment (top row of jacks).
  3. Set the rightmost slider to the middle. It sets the input gain.
  4. Hold the rightmost button and power the rack on. The rightmost slider light blinks green: the module is waiting for audio.
  5. Set the volume high. Turn off sound effects, EQ and notifications. Unplug speakers and headphones: the update sound is harsh.
  6. Play the .wav from the start. The six button lights are a level meter: 3 or 4 lit is right. Move the rightmost slider to get there.
  7. Green slider lights run left to right while it loads. It takes about 80 seconds, then the module restarts on the new firmware.

All six button lights blink red? The level was too low or the audio cut out. Press the rightmost button, raise the gain with the rightmost slider and play the file again from the start. Upside-down module: use the flipped file, with everything mirrored (leftmost segment, button and slider). Coming from qiemem v1.3.0, your segment types and mode are kept. To go back, flash qiemem's v1.3.0 release the same way.
